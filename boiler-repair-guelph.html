Boilers Are Common in Guelph — Boiler Technicians Are Not

Many of Guelph's century homes in the Ward, around Exhibition Park and in the older streets of St. George's Park still heat with hot water radiators, and a growing number of new builds and renovations use in-floor radiant. Both are hydronic systems, and most HVAC companies that service forced-air furnaces do not want to touch them. We do.

A boiler service call starts with the basics: pressure, expansion tank charge, relief valve, pump operation, venting and combustion. Most no-heat calls come down to an ignition component, a stuck pump or a system that lost its pressure through a slow leak somewhere in the loop. We carry the parts for the common residential boilers and can get most systems heating again on the first visit.

No heat and no hot water on a combi boiler? See our no hot water troubleshooting guide. If you also need an emergency plumber for a burst pipe, we handle both on one call.
