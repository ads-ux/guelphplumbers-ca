What to Do Before the Plumber Arrives

Shut off the water. Your main shut-off is usually in the basement where the water line enters the house, beside the water meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops. For a single fixture, use the valve under the sink or behind the toilet.

Kill the power if water is near electrics. If water is reaching outlets, the panel or appliances, switch off the breakers for that area — only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

For a frozen pipe, open the tap it feeds and warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer. Never use an open flame. If the pipe has split, keep the water off until it is repaired.

For a water heater problem that is not leaking, our water heater repair page covers the common faults, and our guide on no hot water in Guelph has troubleshooting you can try first.
