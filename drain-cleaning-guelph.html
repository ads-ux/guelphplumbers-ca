When to Call a Plumber Instead of Reaching for a Bottle

Chemical drain cleaners rarely fix a real blockage and they are hard on the older cast iron and clay drains found in Guelph's pre-1970s neighbourhoods — the Ward, Exhibition Park, St. George's Park and the older streets off Gordon. A single slow sink is usually a quick snake. Several slow drains at once, gurgling when the toilet flushes, or water appearing at the basement floor drain means the main sewer line is involved and needs a plumber with a proper machine.

Tree roots are the most common cause of main-line blockages in Guelph. Mature maples and spruces on older lots find every joint in a clay or cast iron sewer. We clear the roots, run a camera to see how much pipe is affected, and give you a straight answer on whether a yearly cleaning or a repair makes more sense.
