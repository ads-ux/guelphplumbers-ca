A Commercial Plumber Who Works Around Your Business

Commercial plumbing in Guelph is a different job from residential work. The fixtures are heavier duty, the drain lines carry more volume, the codes are stricter and a failure costs you revenue by the hour. We work with restaurants on Macdonell and Wyndham, offices in the Hanlon Business Park, retail in Stone Road Mall's surrounding plazas, and multi-unit residential buildings managed by Guelph property management companies.

We can schedule non-urgent work before opening or after close, and we carry the commercial-grade parts — flush valves, sensor taps, commercial trap primers, grease trap components — that a residential plumber will not have on the truck. Every job is done to the Ontario Building Code and documented for your records and your landlord.

Need a plumber right now? See our 24/7 emergency plumber page. For hot water problems in a building, start with water heater repair, and for recurring blockages see commercial drain cleaning.
