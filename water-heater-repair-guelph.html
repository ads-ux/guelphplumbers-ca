Repair or Replace Your Water Heater?

Most tank water heaters in Guelph last 8 to 12 years. If yours is in that range and the only problem is a pilot, thermocouple or element, repair is usually the right call. If the tank itself is leaking, there is rust in the hot water, or it is past 12 years and starting to rumble, replacement is almost always better value.

Guelph's hard municipal water speeds up wear: scale settles in the bottom of the tank, insulates the burner from the water and makes the heater work harder. That is why a tank that pops and rumbles is often one that has never been flushed.
