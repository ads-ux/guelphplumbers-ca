Rent or Own? What Guelph Homeowners Should Know

A large share of Guelph homes still rent their water heater, often paying more over ten years than three new tanks would cost. If your tank is 10 to 12 years old, rumbling, or has started to leak, replacing it with a unit you own is usually the better math. We handle the rental return paperwork and install the new heater the same day, so you are never without hot water.

Sizing matters more than brand. A family of four with two showers running in the morning needs a different tank — or a properly sized tankless unit — than a couple in a condo. Tankless units in particular are often undersized by installers who do not account for Guelph's winter inlet water temperature, which can drop below 5°C. We size for the worst week of the year, not the brochure.
